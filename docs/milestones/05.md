# Milestone 05: frozen held-out evaluation

## Decision rule fixed before final outcomes

Solve-level validation selected `maximum-bound` as the deterministic comparator
and `ridge` as the primary learned family. Validation used 24 base problems and
32 methods: 768 declared outcomes, 740 eligible numerical runs and 28
unavailable comparisons. All eligible validation artifacts were replayed before
selection. The frozen record preserves all five MLP seeds, including optimizer
iteration-limit warnings; it does not replace the family with its best seed.

The [selection artifact](../../research/protocols/evaluation-selection-v1.json),
[locked executable protocol](../../research/protocols/evaluation-locked-v1.json),
[final freeze](../../research/evaluation-freeze-v1.json) and unchanged
[analysis plan](../ANALYSIS_PLAN.md) distinguish the original broader proposal
from the actual study. The final source/protocol/model/analysis freeze was
recorded before final execution on 17 November 2026, at approximately 10:10 UTC.
Source edits, silent protocol replacement, incomplete paired validation and a
nonempty first-run output directory are rejected by the execution boundary.

The archive declares **3,840 outcomes** and stores **3,700 eligible numerical
payloads**. Its **140 unavailable rows** remain in the manifest and tables.
The alpha-grid reference is scalar-only; the exact PWL transport specialization
supports eligible affine/tent inputs and does not implement nonlinear PSO
preprocessing or a general joint 2D reference. Unavailability has no invented
payload, timing or discrepancy.

The twenty random controls assign seeded priorities to cell paths. They are
not uniform random draws from the active leaf set. All policies preserve the
same complete-cover, atomic replacement, fairness and best/last contracts.

## Estimands and uncertainty

The primary endpoint is the ridge-minus-maximum-bound certification-rate
difference, averaged equally across the four regimes. Every base has equal
weight, and every resource exit or other non-success remains in its
denominator. A reported `CERTIFIED` outcome must meet tolerance over the whole
finite horizon, including time zero.

The fixed 10,000-draw percentile bootstrap resamples complete base groups within
regime and shares each model-seed draw globally across all groups. Ridge has one
frozen fit: this does not estimate uncertainty across independent ridge
training seeds. The analysis preserves the full five-seed MLP family
descriptively. Only the primary certification endpoint is confirmatory under
this protocol; other intervals are descriptive.

Secondary work/time ratios require both members of a pair to certify with
positive cost. Variant ratios are combined inside base/seed pairs, and the
reported statistic is the median of those pair ratios. Work uses all 120 bases;
timing uses only the twelve predefined timing bases. Every ratio reports the
eligible and excluded pair counts. A favorable successful subset cannot
establish an unconditional speedup or a whole-cohort break-even forecast.

Solve time includes learned inference. Post-solve verification has a separate
duration; reference construction, diagnostics, report I/O, plotting and training
are outside solve timing. Cold JVM startup is unmeasured. Five repetitions are
five timings of one base/method configuration, not five new scientific bases.

## Reference and replay boundaries

Six scalar bases have exact-cut staircase diagnostics with reference error
`1/64`. Only a qualified discrepancy's lower endpoint strictly above the
certificate establishes a tested violation. An upper-only exceedance remains
inconclusive. Empty positive cuts have explicit statuses and absent ordinary
Hausdorff distances; they are not recorded as zero error. No joint continuous
2D discrepancy is supplied.

Every eligible payload has an individual SHA-256 over its stored compressed
bytes. Replay loads the saved data, checks the complete declared cohort and
diagnostics, rebuilds coverage and recomputes numerical quantities. The
successful replay receipt binds the actual command exit, verified count,
manifest and frozen fingerprints. This is canonical re-execution with shared
trusted primitives, not an independently implemented proof assistant.

Both primary methods were eligible on every base and had no other failure
status. The equal-regime ridge-minus-maximum-bound difference is **−9.1667
percentage points**, with the frozen grouped 95% percentile interval
**[−14.1667, −4.1667]**. The primary result does not support an improvement from
this learned ranker under the declared budget and cohort.

The conditional median map-image ratio is **1.109589 [1.000000, 1.202470]** on
67/120 paired certifications. The conditional total-time ratio is **1.388827
[1.196698, 1.620587]** on 7/12 predefined timing pairs. The other 53 work pairs
and five timing pairs do not enter those conditional ratios; they remain in
certification and complete outcome tables. Per-regime intervals and unavailable
statistics remain in `primary-effects.csv`. In particular, no OOD 2D primary
pair jointly certified, so a conditional ratio for that regime is unavailable.

The qualified scalar audit contains 1,200 brackets from six bases across
method/time rows, zero verified violations
and 72 upper-only inconclusive exceedances. Absence of a tested violation does
not establish universal numerical correctness. Training break-even is
unavailable because complete comparable pipeline cost is absent.

### A post-hoc illustration, not another endpoint

The lexically first base with disagreeing primary certification outcomes is
`test-id-2d-000`. Maximum-bound reaches tolerance with 317 cells, a bound of
approximately `0.07594013` and 2,532 algorithm map images. Ridge retains its
best bound of approximately `0.12303150` at 444 cells; its last partition has
513 cells and the same bound after exhausting 512 splits, with 4,100 map
images overall. The same certificate recipe remains valid in both runs. This
identifier-selected illustration shows an observed consequence of differing
refinement orders; it does not isolate a causal feature or represent an
additional preregistered test. The complete table retains all eleven
discordant primary bases, all in the same certification direction.
